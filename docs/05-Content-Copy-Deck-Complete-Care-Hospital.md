## 1. Tone & Voice Guide

| Attribute | Guidance |
|---|---|
| Overall tone | Warm, competent, reassuring — never clinical-cold, never overly casual |
| Sentence length | Short to medium; avoid dense medical jargon in patient-facing copy |
| Point of view | "We" for the hospital speaking to "you" the patient/visitor |
| Trust signals | Lead with competence and compassion together, not credentials alone |
| Local grounding | Reference the Gwagwalada/Abuja community naturally, without overdoing it |
| Emergency language | Calm, directive, never alarmist — clear instruction over dramatic framing |
| Avoid | US idioms, US institution names, dollar signs, "911," HIPAA references |
